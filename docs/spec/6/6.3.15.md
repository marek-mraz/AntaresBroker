6.3.15
GeoJSON representation of spatially bound entities
For HTTP GET and POST operations corresponding to "Retrieve Entity" (see clause 5.7.1) and "Query Entities" (see
clause 5.7.2), if the GeoJSON Accept header ("application/geo+json") is present, implementations shall
render the entities of the response in the GeoJSON format, as described in clause 5.2.29.
For GeoJSON representations, a GeoProperty may be selected as the geolocation to be used as the geometry within the
GeoJSON payload. If no geometryProperty parameter is specified, then the location GeoProperty of the Entity is used.
