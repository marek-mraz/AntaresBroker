5.13.5.3
Input data
•
The locally unique identifier that identifies the desired @context in the broker's internal storage. For @contexts
of kind "Cached" this can also be the original URL the broker downloaded the @context from.
•
A reload boolean flag indicating that reloading of the @context shall be attempted (optional).
